# Evidence tiers

## Independent context

Branches with measured targets, adequate evaluation size, no prediction-derived target reuse, and no direct duplicate or strong related-track relationship. These branches are eligible for transfer-facing interpretation.

## Related or proxy support

Branches sharing source structure, sequence overlap, target construction, or intended proxy roles. These branches can support representation and proxy-alignment interpretation, but they do not revise the independent context endpoint.

## Prediction-derived capacity

Branches whose response variable is generated from earlier predictions or confirmation outputs. These branches are retained as capacity checks only.

## Boundary or stress-test branches

Difficult, weak, negative, or sensitivity-only branches retained to expose limits rather than hide failures.
